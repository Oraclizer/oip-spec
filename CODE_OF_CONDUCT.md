# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in OIP should be respectful, technically rigorous and
welcoming. Constructive behavior includes:

- critiquing rules, claims and evidence without attacking people;
- stating assumptions, uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- respecting privacy, attribution and intellectual-property rights;
- helping contributors understand review and test requirements;
- accepting correction and maintainer decisions professionally.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private vulnerabilities,
personal attacks, sustained disruption and retaliation against good-faith
reporting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation or
take other proportionate action when behavior threatens contributors, the
integrity of the project or security. Decisions consider context, impact,
repetition and whether earlier direction was followed.

Report conduct concerns privately by email to `jay@oraclizer.io`, the
contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include unrelated personal
data. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical, but no absolute
confidentiality or response time is guaranteed.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the
project in public.
